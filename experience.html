How an evening unfolds.

    Arrival by the hearth

  2. 19:30

    The first courses

    Starters arrive in the order the kitchen thinks they are best, paced to the conversation.

  3. 20:30

    From the fire

    Mains are sliced at the pass and brought straight to you. Wine pairings poured at the table.

    The pastry room

    Dessert, then a single-origin espresso — and an open invitation to visit the cellar.
