The Signature Experience

Six layers.
One perfect bite.

Keep scrolling — you are directing the film.

  1. The crown

    Sesame brioche

    Baked each morning with cultured butter, glazed twice, crowned with toasted sesame.

  2. Freshness

    Butter lettuce

    Ice-crisp leaves add snap and cool the richness below.

  3. Acidity

    Heirloom tomato

    Sliced to order, seasoned with flaked salt, never refrigerated.

  4. Richness

    Aged cheddar

    Eighteen-month cheddar, melted under a cloche until it drapes.

  5. Depth

    Dry-aged beef

    A coarse-ground blend aged in our salt chamber, seared hard on cast iron.

  6. Structure

    Toasted base

    A buttered, griddled heel that holds every layer until the final bite.
